# Gen 2 Frame Art Briefs

Concept art briefs for all 27 Generation 2 mecha frames in the No Haven on Earth Atlas.

## How to use this file (instructions for ChatGPT)

- Generate one image per request. Use the frame's **Scene prompt** for an illustration or its **Turnaround prompt** for a design sheet.
- Each prompt is self-contained: it already includes the frame description, the maker's house style, the setting-wide style and an "Avoid" list. Use it as written.
- Keep every image in the same style: grounded real-robot mecha concept art, landscape format (16:9 for scenes), no text or logos baked into the image.
- The briefs are the source of truth. If a prompt and a brief disagree, follow the brief.
- **Scale:** a person is about 1.8 m, roughly one tenth of a frame's height. On turnaround sheets the human figure should reach only to the frame's ankle or lower shin. Earlier images drew people far too large, which makes the frames read at half size.
- Ascension frames (AW-5, AW-5S) have four arms on purpose.

## Art direction for the whole setting

- Real robot, not super robot. Frames are 16–21 m military machines that look buildable, serviceable and mass-produced.
- Show the power plant. Exhausts, battery spines, fuel-cell stacks and capacitor housings are part of each silhouette.
- No glowing energy outside prototypes. Sensors may glow faintly; weapons do not.
- Always give scale: trucks, infantry, ground crew, buildings or trees.
- Paint, stencils and wear tell the story. These frames have seen hard use.
- Reference points: Gundam 00 season 1, Patlabor, Votoms, Front Mission and SRW OG's Gespenst line.

**Setting style (included in every prompt):** grounded real-robot mecha concept art, believable military hardware, visible joints, hydraulic pistons and armour panel lines, stencilled markings, realistic weathering with chipped paint and grime, clear scale reference with vehicles or people nearby, early-generation technology with no glowing energy effects, detailed industrial design, cinematic lighting.

**Avoid (included in every prompt):** super robot proportions, glowing energy wings, magic effects, chrome, organic or biomechanical forms, fantasy armour, cape, oversized head, chibi, cute, extra limbs, deformed hands, blurry, text, watermark, logo.

## Halcyon Aeronautics

*United States · Air Force*

Fighter jets that learned to stand. Thin, angular and tall, with swept wing binders behind the shoulders and a single slit visor. Panels follow aircraft logic: intakes, control surfaces, canopy-like chest glass. Navy blue and white, with high-visibility orange used sparingly. Markings are aviation-style: squadron numbers, low-visibility roundels, NO STEP and rescue-arrow stencils.

**House style:** Halcyon Aeronautics design language: slender angular mech derived from fighter jets, swept wing binders, single horizontal slit visor, aircraft intakes and control surfaces, navy blue and white with small high-visibility orange accents, aviation stencils and squadron numbers.

### HX-7 Kestrel

Gen 2 · early · 18.0 m · 36 t · Variable-frame strike fighter; America's signature mech

- **Silhouette:** Tall, narrow and long-legged, with a slight forward lean. Two swept wing binders rise behind the shoulders in a shallow V.
- **Head:** Narrow wedge head that folds into the nose cone in flight mode. One amber horizontal slit visor and a short blade antenna.
- **Torso:** Slim chest with a tinted canopy-like panel and air intakes on each flank. The cockpit sits behind the chest panel.
- **Arms:** Thin angular arms. The right forearm houses a folding VB-2 vibro knife; the blade is visible as a seam along the forearm.
- **Legs:** Long legs whose calves become engine nacelles in flight mode, with exhaust nozzles at the heels. Small feet with toe claws.
- **Back & power:** QX quick-swap battery spine along the back with yellow crane hard points and handles. The battery drives electric ducted fans in the calf nacelles, so the heel nozzles are fan outlets: no combustion exhaust, flame or soot. It is quiet when parked and far quieter than a jet in flight, but not silent.
- **Weapons:** LR-3 linear rifle: long and slim with a boxy capacitor stock and twin rails along the barrel, cooling fins near the muzzle. AGM-M missiles, two under each wing binder.
- **Colours:** Navy blue upper body, white lower legs and wing undersides, high-visibility orange wingtips and walkway stripes. Low-visibility roundel and a two-digit squadron number on the leg.
- **Details:** NO STEP stencils on the wings, rescue arrow near the cockpit, scuffing around the battery handles, heat discolouration on the rifle rails.
- **Shot:** Just landed on a closed highway in morning haze, knees bent, wings still unfolding, dust kicked up, a ground-crew truck in the background.

### HX-11 Harrier

- **Arms:** Heavier forearms with the folding VB-2 knife. Extra armour on the upper arms.
- **Legs:** Same nacelle legs as the Kestrel, with larger nozzles and armour skirts at the hips.
- **Back & power:** QX-3 high-density battery pack with a ribbed capacitor buffer. Larger wing binders with three hardpoints each.
- **Weapons:** LR-3 linear rifle. PL-2 point-defence laser in a small domed turret on the left shoulder with a dark lens. Six AGM-M missiles under the wings.
- **Colours:** Darker navy blue with medium grey lower body. Orange kept for wingtips only. Kill markings under the cockpit.
- **Details:** The laser lens has a protective iris shutter. Scorch marks from near-miss rockets on the shoulder armour.
- **Shot:** Low pass over a desert valley at dusk, wings swept, a missile leaving the rail, small explosions in the sky where the laser has hit incoming rockets.

## Brannock-Hale Heavy Industries

*United States · Army*

Tanks with legs. Boxy, front-heavy armour with sloped glacis plates, crouched legs and shoulder weapon pods. Everything looks like it can be fixed with a wrench: grab handles, tow hooks, tie-down points, jerrycans and whip antennas. Big louvred turbine vents on the back. Olive drab or desert tan with stencilled serials.

**House style:** Brannock-Hale design language: boxy tank-like mech, sloped front armour glacis, crouched heavy legs, shoulder weapon pods, louvred turbine exhaust vents on the back, grab handles, tow hooks and whip antennas, olive drab military paint with white stencilled serial numbers.

- **Legs:** Heavy crouched legs with knee armour, mud on the feet and tie-down rings at the ankles.
- **Back & power:** HT-12 turbine-diesel hybrid with louvred turbine exhaust and an armoured battery box. Jerrycans, a tow cable and a pick-axe-sized tool rack.
- **Weapons:** AR-40 Longhorn assault rifle with underbarrel grenade launcher. Hornet 12-cell missile pod on the right shoulder. M140 rocket bazooka slung on the back.
- **Colours:** Olive drab with black-green disruptive camo, white stencilled serial, unit insignia on the shoulder pod.
- **Details:** Mud spatter, faded paint on the glacis, antenna whips with tape, crew name painted near the hatch.
- **Shot:** A fire team of four moving through a pine forest on a mountain slope in drizzle, one kneeling to aim the bazooka, mud and broken trees.
