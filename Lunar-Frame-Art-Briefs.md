# Lunar Frame Art Briefs

Concept art briefs for the three Shackleton Works frames in the No Haven on Earth Atlas: the frames the Lunar Republic builds on the Moon for Earth's gravity and gives to the UN's white squads. The LW-3 Tellus and LW-5 Albedo sit in the **Gen 2 · late** column; the LW-X Earthrise sits in **Prototype · In testing**.

## How to use this file (instructions for ChatGPT)

- Generate one image per request. Use the frame's **Scene prompt** for an illustration or its **Turnaround prompt** for a design sheet.
- Each prompt is self-contained: it already includes the frame description, the maker's house style, the setting-wide style and an "Avoid" list. Use it as written.
- Keep every image in the same style as the Gen 1, Gen 2 and prototype sets: grounded real-robot mecha concept art, landscape format (16:9 for scenes), no text or logos baked into the image.
- The briefs are the source of truth. If a prompt and a brief disagree, follow the brief.
- If the AW-5 Rigger image is attached for reference (`output/imagegen/` Rigger files), keep a faint family resemblance in the joints and cockpit. These are Ascension worker-frame engineering grown up and built for Earth, not a new bloc's war machine.
- **Scale:** a person is about 1.8 m, roughly one tenth of a frame's height. On turnaround sheets the human figure should reach only to the frame's ankle or lower shin.
- **UN white:** every frame is painted UN white with a pale blue head. Nothing is camouflaged, and none of them carries a bloc's markings.
- **Shared head design:** Albedo's angular hinged visor is the common Shackleton Works head language across Tellus, Albedo and Earthrise: pale blue wedge, narrow horizontal optical slit, small brow and visible side hinges. Tellus keeps its paired rescue searchlights; Earthrise keeps its upward-facing receiver dish. Albedo alone adds heavy heat shielding. No humanoid mouth or central mono-eye.
- None of these frames has an energy effect. The Earthrise's power beam is invisible.

## What makes a Shackleton frame different

- **Built for a gravity they don't have.** Lighter and slimmer than bloc frames, with oversized joints and foot pads that show the engineers over-built for a load they could never test at home.
- **Sealed.** Every cockpit has its own air supply: visible air tanks, seal lines round the canopy, and filters for smoke, gas and fallout.
- **Rescue first.** A rescue cradle or capsule in the chest, plus searchlights, winches and handholds for people to climb. Weapons are small and were fitted by Geneva afterwards, so they look like add-ons.
- **Vacuum-cast armour.** Smooth, slightly porous titanium-foam panels with fewer seams than bloc frames, and a faint grey regolith dust in the panel lines.

## Art direction for the whole setting

- Real robot, not super robot. Frames are 15–21 m machines that look buildable and serviceable.
- Show the power plant. Fuel-cell stacks, ice-water tanks, radiators and the Earthrise's rectenna are part of each silhouette.
- Always give scale: trucks, people, ground crew, buildings or trees.
- Paint, stencils and wear tell the story.

**Setting style (included in every prompt):** grounded real-robot mecha concept art, believable hardware, visible joints, hydraulic pistons and armour panel lines, stencilled markings, realistic weathering, clear scale reference with vehicles or people nearby, detailed industrial design, cinematic lighting.

**Avoid (included in every prompt):** super robot proportions, glowing energy wings, glowing weapons, magic effects, chrome, organic or biomechanical forms, fantasy armour, cape, oversized head, chibi, cute, extra limbs, deformed hands, blurry, text, watermark, logo, copies of existing anime designs.

### LW-3 Tellus

Gen 2 · late · 16.8 m · 41 t · Luna's first frame built for Earth, and the Republic's gift to the white squads in 2183.

- **Silhouette:** Slim and upright, lighter than any bloc frame of its size, with thick knee and ankle joints and wide foot pads. It reads as careful rather than aggressive.
- **Head:** Compact, pale blue, with the common Albedo-derived hinged wedge visor, straight narrow horizontal optical slit, slight brow and visible side hinges. A wide sensor band sits behind the protective visor, with paired rescue searchlights at the sides and a small UN beacon on top.
- **Torso:** Rounded chest that hinges open into a rescue cradle with padded straps for several people. Seal lines round a domed cockpit canopy above it. Air tanks in recessed bays on the shoulders.
- **Arms:** Rigger-derived arms with strong hands, a winch on the left forearm and handholds along the arm for people to climb.
- **Legs:** Long, with oversized joints and broad pads, built for a load the engineers could only guess at.
- **Back & power:** An ice-water fuel-cell stack in a smooth backpack, with two radiator fins.
- **Weapons:** A Mitsurugi Type-L coil carbine on a sling, fitted by Geneva and visibly not part of the original design, and a TH-2 tether harpoon on the right forearm.
- **Colours:** UN white with a pale blue head, a small stencilled UN number on the shoulder, grey lunar dust in the panel lines and fresh Earth mud on the feet.
- **Details:** A Shackleton Works builder's plate, a gift tag stencilled on the hip in Republic lettering, desert dust from the Atacama trials.
- **Shot:** The riverbank at Herat at dusk. The Tellus kneels in shallow water with its chest cradle open, people climbing in, while smoke rises over the city behind. In the far distance a vague giant silhouette stands on a bridge.

### LW-5 Albedo

Gen 2 · late · 17.4 m · 47 t · A Tellus rebuilt for wildfires: it cuts firebreaks and walks through heat that would cook a crew in any other frame.

- **Silhouette:** A Tellus bulked out with layered heat-shield panels and a big water bladder on the back. Lower and broader in the shoulders.
- **Head:** The family's reference head: pale blue angular wedge behind a hinged heat visor, straight narrow horizontal optical slit, slight brow, visible side hinge blocks and smooth faceted cheeks. No humanoid mouth or central mono-eye.
- **Torso:** Silvered ceramic heat-shield panels over the chest, scorched and sooty, with the rescue cradle behind an insulated door.
- **Arms:** Left arm carries trenching cutters on a folding boom. Right hand holds a fire monitor nozzle fed from the back.
- **Legs:** Tellus legs with heat skirts over the knees and a dozer blade that folds down in front of the shins.
- **Back & power:** Fuel-cell stack under insulation, two large water bladders and a hose reel, plus an intake pipe for filling from a river.
- **Weapons:** None.
- **Colours:** UN white with a pale blue head, but most of the frame is silvered heat shielding blackened by soot. Red fire-service hazard bands on the blade and boom.
- **Details:** Ash on every flat surface, scorched paint, a Union fire-service loan stencil on one shoulder.
- **Shot:** A Mediterranean hillside wildfire at night. The Albedo cuts a firebreak through burning scrub with its blade down and its nozzle spraying, a village behind it, fire crews and trucks small at its feet.

### LW-X Earthrise

Prototype · 17.0 m · 39 t · A Tellus rebuilt around a rectenna for beamed power from the Republic's relay satellites, built for the best pilots in the white squads. Two exist.

- **Silhouette:** The slimmest of the three: a Tellus with a large flat rectenna panel folded on its back like a closed book, opening upward into a wide dish. Lighter armour, longer legs.

- **Arms:** Clean Tellus arms with a tether harpoon on the right forearm.
- **Legs:** Longer and lighter than the Tellus, still with broad pads.
- **Back & power:** The rectenna, a power-conditioning box beneath it, and Tellus fuel cells as a backup.
- **Weapons:** A lunar-built coil carbine, neater than the Tellus's add-on, and the tether harpoon.
- **Colours:** UN white and pale blue, with prototype calibration markers (black-and-white quartered circles) and bare primer on the rectenna hinges.
- **Details:** A line of small stencilled lettering inside the open cockpit hatch, too small to read. Instrumentation pods on the hips. Atacama dust.
- **Shot:** The Atacama trials at dawn. The Earthrise stands on salt flats with its rectenna open to the sky, the Moon pale and low on the horizon, test trucks and engineers around it, and a second white frame watching from a distance.

- **Identity:** Retains the Earthrise skeleton, long legs, oversized joints, broad foot pads and smooth lunar-cast armour.
- **Head:** Shared Albedo-derived pale blue angular hinged visor, narrow horizontal optical slit and visible side hinges. No head dish, mono-eye or respirator.
- **Protection:** Layered ceramic-composite plates on chest, shoulders, upper arms, hips, knees and shins; reinforced joints and bracing.
- **Cockpit and rescue:** Opaque armoured cockpit door; no visible pilot. Protected four-person rescue capsule remains behind its own hatch, with beacon and evacuation handholds.
- **Shield:** Large left-arm ballistic shelter shield, chamfered rectangular face with replaceable impact plates, protected sensor slit, forearm yoke and ground-bracing foot.
- **Weapon:** Larger right-hand heavy lunar coil rifle with shrouded accelerator barrel, box magazine and protected power feed; right-forearm tether harpoon retained.
- **Power:** Rectenna folded compactly behind the torso in combat; protected power conditioning and backup fuel cells retained. No visible power beam.
- **Finish:** UN white, pale blue head and shield band, chipped edges, soot and fresh mud. No faction camouflage or heraldry.
- **Scene:** Holds the entrance to a riverside hospital during evacuation at dawn, shield between incoming fire and civilians boarding buses and ambulances.
