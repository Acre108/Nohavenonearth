# Super Robot Art Briefs

Concept art briefs for the super robots in the No Haven on Earth Atlas (the **Supers** tree): seventeen giants and one hunting pack from twelve programs, plus the Specimen the Martian line descends from. Supers appear late in Gen 2. Each one answers the same question: what can the human spirit be made to do?

**Revision, 19 October 2026.** Eight new entries (12–19): the Type-30 Jingwei, Morevna, the Staya, Roland, Aurora, the Custodian, the Counterfeit and the Tramuntana. H-46 gets an optional new scene, because it has now chosen a pilot. Koschei-2 gets a text update only.

**Revision, 18 October 2026.** Following the design review: the Halden giants take accession designations (H-17, H-24 / H-31, H-46), pilots ride in an armoured cab on an engineered restraint exoframe, and the umbilical power cable becomes a perfusion and cooling bundle. The Jinn loses its human head and outside rider. Concord is redesigned to look like the machine that started the super race. Koschei pilot limits and Koschei-2 access are clarified. Entries marked **Regenerate** need new images; the old images and prompts remain in `output/imagegen/supers` for reference.

## How to use this file (instructions for ChatGPT)

- Generate one image per request. Use the entry's **Scene prompt** for an illustration or its **Turnaround prompt** for a design sheet.
- Each prompt is self-contained: it already includes the description, the program's house style, the supers style and an "Avoid" list. Use it as written.
- Keep every image in the same family as the Gen 1, Gen 2 and prototype sets: grounded concept art, landscape format (16:9 for scenes), no text or logos baked into the image.
- The briefs are the source of truth. If a prompt and a brief disagree, follow the brief.
- The Martian line (the Specimen, the Halden giants and the Jinn) is partly organic on purpose. Every other super is pure machinery.

## What makes a super different from a real robot

Supers are still drawn as real hardware in a real world. What changes is scale and one uncanny element.

- **Scale first.** Supers are 36–50 m, two to three times the height of a normal 17 m frame. Every image needs a normal mech, vehicles or buildings nearby so the size reads at once. The one exception is the Staya: six normal-sized frames built to hunt giants, so its images reverse the scale and put a giant over them.
- **One uncanny element per program, everything else engineering.** Halden: the grown Martian tissue. Department Zero: the sealed pilot capsule. Tsukuyomi: the reactor fins that glow under load and the shrine around it. Vireo: the eleven minds, shown as eleven sensor lenses. Joint Heavy Systems has no uncanny element: it is real-robot technology pushed too far, and it should look like it.
- **Sombre, not triumphant.** These machines cost people: pilots used up, bonded, replaced or already dead. Prefer quiet, heavy, ominous framing to heroic poses. The Jinn and the Choir are the exceptions: they protect people, so their scenes can be hopeful, but never glamorous.
- **Energy effects only where the brief names them.** Takemikazuchi's fins and beam rifle may glow. Nothing else does.
- **Made, maintained and moved.** Show the infrastructure: perfusion bundles, gantries, reactor carriages, rail cars, hangars, support crews.
- Reference points: Neon Genesis Evangelion (the restraint-armour idea only, not its designs), Pacific Rim's sense of weight, Gunbuster's scale, Patlabor's maintenance realism, Giant Robo's ominous framing. Copy no existing designs.

**Supers style (included in every prompt):** grounded super robot concept art, a giant treated as real hardware, believable heavy engineering, visible joints, armour panel lines, maintenance hatches and stencilled markings, realistic weathering, massive scale shown with normal-sized 17 metre mechs, vehicles, buildings or people nearby, sombre cinematic lighting.

**Avoid (included in every prompt):** heroic posing, glossy toy-like finish, chrome, magic effects, glowing energy wings, fantasy armour, cape, chibi, cute, deformed hands, blurry, text, watermark, logo, copies of existing anime designs. Non-Martian entries also add "organic or biomechanical forms". Entries without an allowed energy effect also add "glowing weapons, glowing eyes".

## The Martian line

*One alien source, treated two opposite ways*

Everything Martian shares one material: a grey-black tissue, partly mineralised, like dark stone, bone and sinew fused together. It is neither clearly alive nor dead. It reacts to people nearby, and more strongly to fear, anger and grief. Draw it as dense, fibrous and cracked, with a faint wet sheen in the seams; never as glowing, slimy or monstrous. The Halden giants hide most of it inside clinical restraint frames; the Jinn shows more of it, between League armour panels.

**Halden house style:** Halden Institute design language: a giant grown body of grey-black partly mineralised tissue like dark stone, bone and sinew fused together, enclosed in a clinical white and pale grey external restraint exoframe of load-bearing steel trusses, armour panels, articulated joint brakes and numbered inspection plates, an armoured operator cab with vision blocks and a side hatch built into the chest of the frame, contact interface pads where the frame meets the tissue, a thick perfusion and cooling bundle from the upper back, Swiss medical-engineering precision, hazard and inspection markings.

The tissue moves itself. Halden's support equipment regulates it: the bundle carries perfusion fluid, cooling and the suppression cycle, not electrical power. Off the bundle, a giant lasts as long as its heat and metabolite load allow.

### The Specimen

- **Condition:** Cracked, eroded by Martian dust, one arm broken off at the elbow. It looks ancient.
- **Uncanny cue:** Nothing moves. The only sign of activity is on the monitors around it.
- **Setting:** Station Nine's sealed containment bay: a huge cylindrical chamber in orbit, the body held in a steel cradle by padded clamps, surrounded by scaffolding, sample drills and banks of monitors. Researchers in clean suits look tiny beside it.
- **Shot:** A researcher in a clean suit stands on a catwalk at the level of the Specimen's face. On the monitor bank behind her, the activity traces have spiked.

### Halden H-24 / H-31 — Field Cohort

**Revised 18 Oct 2026 · Regenerate scene and turnaround.** Replaces "Halden Units 01–02". Restraint armour becomes an external exoframe with an operator cab; the umbilical power cable becomes a perfusion and cooling bundle; the five-minute battery becomes a heat and metabolite limit.

Gen 2 · late · 41 m · The spirit as leash. Two separately grown organisms in the same pattern of restraint frame, each bonded to a pilot Halden chose for psychological profile, not skill. A giant is calm only while its pilot is connected through the cab. The tissue moves itself; the bundle from the grid carries perfusion, cooling and the suppression cycle. Off the bundle it lasts as long as its heat and metabolite load allow: minutes in a hard fight.

- **Form:** An external restraint exoframe encloses most of the body: load-bearing white steel trusses running down every limb, bolted armour panels over the chest, back and thighs, and visible load paths into the feet. It should read first as engineered military hardware, with the grown tissue showing only in the gaps between frame members and at the joints.
- **Head:** The faceless tissue head is enclosed in an armoured sensor housing that is part of the frame: a squared white visor plate with camera clusters, a short antenna mast, and clamps locking it to the frame collar. It reads as a machine head.

- **Support:** The tissue is its own engine. A thick perfusion and cooling bundle runs from the upper back to a tracked Halden support vehicle carrying pumps and coolant tanks. Coolant reserve tanks and heat-exchanger panels sit on the lower back, with a temperature gauge (no readable numbers).
- **Differences:** H-24 is the standard fit. H-31 matures faster and runs hotter: extra bolted radiator packs on both shoulders and a second coolant tank on the lower back. Otherwise the same frame pattern; they differ by more than a painted number.
- **Weapons:** None built in. It fights with its body and whatever it can lift. Halden supplies no guns.
- **Colours:** Clinical white and pale grey with black stencilled accession numbers, yellow-and-black hazard bands at the joint brakes.
- **Shot:** A night operation in an alpine valley. ### The Jinn

**Revised 18 Oct 2026 · Regenerate scene and turnaround.** The pilot rides inside an armoured chest cab, not on the back. The head has no human features. It is visibly maintained, with supply couplings in place of the old cable.

Gen 2 · late · 40 m · The spirit as partner. H-17, after its restraint frame shattered and its pilot died in the Great Sand Sea war. A League mechanic worked out what Halden never had: the suppression cycle caused the distress Halden treated as aggression. With the cycle cut, it stopped fighting. The mechanic rebuilt the wrecked cab around a scavenged Sirocco cockpit and pilots it from inside the chest. With no grid bundle it lives on water, feedstock and rest, delivered by League Kafila carriers. Its pilot wants to help people.

- **Form:** H-17's body inside what's left of its first-pattern exoframe, rebuilt in League workshop style. The chest and back sections survive, repaired with welded plate and mining-rig hydraulics; the broken limb bands are cut away, and League frame armour panels are bolted onto repaired truss sections down the arms and legs. More covered and more machine-like than before, with weathered tissue showing only between the panels.
