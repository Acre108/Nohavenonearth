# Variant Frame Art Briefs

Concept art briefs for the 25 named variant and custom frames introduced on the No Haven on Earth Atlas's Aces tab. Each one is a version of a frame that already has a brief in the Gen 1, Gen 2 or Gen 3 files. It should be recognisable as that frame at a glance, with the listed changes.

## How to use this file (instructions for ChatGPT)

- Generate one image per request. Use the frame's **Scene prompt** for an illustration or its **Turnaround prompt** for a design sheet.
- Each prompt is self-contained: it already includes the frame description, the maker's house style, the era style where it applies, the setting-wide style and an "Avoid" list. Use it as written.
- Keep every image in the same style as the existing sets: grounded real-robot mecha concept art, landscape format (16:9 for scenes), no text or logos baked into the image.
- If an image of the base frame is attached, match it closely and change only what the brief lists. These are variants, not new designs.
- The briefs are the source of truth. If a prompt and a brief disagree, follow the brief.
- **Scale:** a person is about 1.8 m, roughly one tenth of a frame's height. On turnaround sheets the human figure should reach only to the frame's ankle or lower shin.
- Frames marked Gen 1 use the Gen 1 era style: older, cruder, bolted and riveted, with a world a generation older around them.
- The Lifter and Rigger variants have four arms on purpose.

### M6 Longstride

Gen 1 · trials, 2167 · 19.5 m · 52 t · Lighter, longer-legged Garrison for jungle and mud; eight built · Flown by Wendell Sykes

- Built on the M4 Garrison. Same tank-hull body, cupola head and turbine, so it must read as a Garrison at a glance.
- Legs about a third longer, with an extra-long shin and huge splayed duck-foot pads half again as wide as the M4's.
- About a third of the side armour plates removed, so the frame and hydraulics show through the gaps.
- No shoulder missile tubes. An added radiator grille beside the turbine exhaust for tropical heat.
- Yellow trial-unit bands on the arms and legs over faded olive drab. Mud caked to the thighs.
- **Shot:** Wading out of a flooded jungle river in Mindanao while an ordinary M4 Garrison sits stuck to its knees in the mud behind it. Trial engineers with clipboards on the bank beside an old army truck.
