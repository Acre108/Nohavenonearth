# Gen 3 Prototype Art Briefs

Concept art briefs for all 12 prototype frames in the No Haven on Earth Atlas: the machines in testing now that will become the next generation. In the Atlas these sit in the **Prototype · In testing** column, after Gen 2 · late.

## How to use this file (instructions for ChatGPT)

- Generate one image per request. Use the frame's **Scene prompt** for an illustration or its **Turnaround prompt** for a design sheet.
- Each prompt is self-contained: it already includes the frame description, the maker's house style, the prototype style, the setting-wide style and an "Avoid" list. Use it as written.
- Keep every image in the same style as the Gen 1 and Gen 2 sets: grounded real-robot mecha concept art, landscape format (16:9 for scenes), no text or logos baked into the image.
- The briefs are the source of truth. If a prompt and a brief disagree, follow the brief.
- If the Gen 2 parent's image is attached for reference, keep the family resemblance clear. Each prototype is its parent rebuilt around one new technology, not a new design.
- **Scale:** a person is about 1.8 m, roughly one tenth of a frame's height. On turnaround sheets the human figure should reach only to the frame's ankle or lower shin. Earlier images drew people far too large, which makes the frames read at half size.
- The AWX-1 Marlinspike has no legs on purpose.
- The Type-20 Tianyan is a command frame with six uncrewed escorts, and the NK-X Ashvin is a matched pair. Show all of them; the group is the point of the image.
- The BD-X Polaris carries no weapons on purpose.

## What makes a prototype different from Gen 2

Each prototype exists to test one new technology, so the image should make that one thing obvious.

- **One new thing, built in.** A beamed-power receiver, a turbine-capacitor plant, a plasma blade, a siege gun, a fission reactor, a beam rifle, a space-combat airframe. It is the largest, newest-looking part of the frame. Everything else is the familiar Gen 2 parent.
- **Unfinished.** Test-unit paint, calibration markers (black-and-white quartered circles), instrumentation pods, cable bundles taped down, access panels left open, parts in bare primer.
- **Very few exist.** One or two of each. They are guarded, watched by engineers, and followed by support trucks.
- **Energy effects are allowed, but only where the brief names them.** The Raijin's beam, the Saetta's plasma blade and the Tsar's particle cannon may glow. Nothing else does. Even the allowed effects look dangerous and hard to control, never magical.
- **Neater than Gen 1 and 2, but still real hardware.** Tighter panel lines and newer materials next to old service parts. It should never look like a super robot.

## Art direction for the whole setting

- Real robot, not super robot. Frames are 15–21 m military machines that look buildable and serviceable.
- Show the power plant. Exhausts, battery spines, fuel-cell stacks, capacitor housings, heat sinks and reactor shielding are part of each silhouette.
- Always give scale: trucks, infantry, ground crew, engineers, buildings or trees.
- Paint, stencils and wear tell the story.
- Reference points: Gundam 00 season 1, Mobile Suit Gundam: The 08th MS Team, Patlabor, Front Mission and SRW OG's Gespenst and R-series prototypes. Take the feel only; copy no existing designs.

**Prototype style (included in every prompt):** experimental prototype mech currently in testing, its service parent rebuilt around one new technology, test-unit paint with black and white calibration markers and instrumentation pods, cable bundles and open access panels, hand-built one-off parts in bare primer beside worn service parts.

**Setting style (included in every prompt):** grounded real-robot mecha concept art, believable military hardware, visible joints, hydraulic pistons and armour panel lines, stencilled markings, realistic weathering, clear scale reference with vehicles or people nearby, detailed industrial design, cinematic lighting.

**Avoid (included in every prompt):** super robot proportions, glowing energy wings, magic effects, chrome, organic or biomechanical forms, fantasy armour, cape, oversized head, chibi, cute, extra limbs, deformed hands, blurry, text, watermark, logo, copies of existing anime designs. (Frames without an allowed energy effect also add "glowing weapons". The Marlinspike's prompt drops "extra limbs" and adds "legs".)

## Halcyon Aeronautics

*United States · Air Force*

**House style:** Halcyon Aeronautics design language: slender angular mech derived from fighter jets, swept wing binders, single horizontal slit visor, aircraft intakes and control surfaces, navy blue and white with small high-visibility orange accents, aviation stencils and squadron numbers.

### HX-X SKYLINE Testbed

Prototype · 18.4 m · 43 t · Harrier with a microwave power receiver. Two exist; one has flown for six hours without landing.

- **Silhouette:** A Harrier (broad shoulders, big swept wing binders, add-on chest armour) with a large, flat, segmented rectenna panel on the back, raised on struts like a radar dish laid flat. It makes the outline wider and flatter from above.
- **Head:** Harrier wedge head with slit visor, plus an extra data-link antenna and a pitot boom for flight-test data.
- **Torso:** Harrier chest with an instrumentation pod bolted under the cockpit and thick power cables running from the rectenna into the battery housing.
- **Arms:** Standard Harrier arms. LR-3 linear rifle in hand.
- **Legs:** Harrier nacelle legs, with test-camera pods on the hips filming the wing binders.
- **Back & power:** The rectenna: a grid of small antenna elements in a dark lattice, with heat-dissipation fins at the edges. QX-3 high-density pack underneath. A faint heat shimmer rises from the rectenna when it is receiving.
- **Weapons:** LR-3 linear rifle. PL-2 point-defence laser turret on the left shoulder.
- **Colours:** White test-airframe paint with navy blue sections, high-visibility orange flight-test bands and black-and-white calibration markers on the wings.
- **Details:** A flight-hours tally painted under the cockpit, sun-bleached paint on the top surfaces, small warning chevrons around the rectenna.
- **Shot:** High above a sea of clouds at sunset, in flight mode with wings swept, the rectenna angled up. A tiny bright point of a relay satellite sits in the darkening sky above. The power beam itself is invisible: show only a faint shimmer above the rectenna. A chase aircraft flies alongside.

## Brannock-Hale Heavy Industries

*United States · Army*

**House style:** Brannock-Hale design language: boxy tank-like mech, sloped front armour glacis, crouched heavy legs, shoulder weapon pods, louvred turbine exhaust vents on the back, grab handles, tow hooks and whip antennas, olive drab military paint with white stencilled serial numbers.

### M15 Vanguard

Prototype · 17.8 m · 66 t · Testbed for the turbine-capacitor plant. The Army's attempt to match linear-weapon penetration without depending on Halcyon.

- **Silhouette:** A Teamster (chunky, wide-shouldered, crouched, front-heavy) with a noticeably bigger back: a capacitor bank grafted onto the turbine housing. The long electrothermal rifle sticks out well past the body.
- **Head:** Teamster cupola head with an added fire-control laser rangefinder box and a test-data antenna.
- **Torso:** Teamster glacis, with heavy armoured power conduits running over the shoulder to the rifle. A cooling-air intake scoop on the right side.
- **Arms:** Right arm reinforced with recoil dampers for the rifle. Left forearm carries the PB-4 stake driver with its six-shot cylinder of explosive blanks.
- **Legs:** Teamster legs, unchanged.
- **Back & power:** HT-20 turbine-capacitor plant: the familiar louvred turbine vents, with a boxy, ribbed capacitor bank stacked above them (bare metal, with Mitsurugi-style precise panelling that doesn't match the rest of the frame), and orange high-voltage cable runs.
- **Weapons:** AR-40E electrothermal rifle: a long, heavy rifle with a thick barrel shroud, a capacitor module under the barrel and a power cable into the stock. On firing, a brief white-violet plasma flash at the muzzle and a lot of propellant smoke. No beam.
- **Colours:** Olive drab with yellow test-unit bands on the arms and legs, calibration markers on the shoulders, the capacitor bank in bare metal and primer.
- **Details:** Scorched muzzle shroud, high-voltage warning stencils, an engineer's clipboard tied to the leg, tape over cable joints.
- **Shot:** Field trials on a desert proving ground. The Vanguard fires from a kneeling brace at a row of armour-plate targets, with a plasma flash at the muzzle and a shock ring kicking up dust. Engineers watch from behind a sandbagged instrument trailer.

## Atelier Castellani

*Continental Union · VEK subsidiary*

**House style:** Atelier Castellani design language: elegant slim knightly mech, sculpted flowing armour like an Italian sports car, hand-finished details, deep red paint with gold trim and pinstriping, personal ace emblem.

### CA-X Saetta

Prototype · 18.5 m · 45 t · Plasma-blade prototype for the Union's top ace. It fights at full power for about forty seconds.

- **Silhouette:** A Falco (slim, long-limbed, sculpted) with heavier, braced forearms and upper arms borrowed from the Destrier, so the arms look slightly too strong for the body. A bulge in the chest for the capacitor core.
- **Head:** Falco head with a mono-eye on a track behind a narrow visor, and a swept crest. Cooling slots on the cheeks.
- **Torso:** Sculpted chest with a raised, vented housing over the licensed capacitor core, and a heat-discoloured exhaust grille beneath it.
- **Arms:** Destrier-style bracing on both arms: external struts and hydraulic dampers to take the blade's reaction forces. Thick power cables run down the right arm to the hilt.
- **Legs:** Long Falco legs with sculpted thigh armour and racing-style cooling ducts.
- **Back & power:** Stack-R racing fuel cells in polished housings, with a large heat-sink fin pack added for the blade.
- **Weapons:** Lama Ardente plasma blade: a heavy hilt with capacitor modules and magnetic emitter rings; when lit, a straight blade of contained white-orange plasma, crackling and unstable at the edges. G35 telescoped rifle stowed on the hip.
- **Colours:** Deep red with gold trim and pinstriping, the ace's personal emblem on the shoulder, test-unit calibration markers in gold rather than black and white. Heat-blued metal around the emitters.
- **Details:** Scorch streaks on the forearms, hand-finished rivets, a small stopwatch-style countdown gauge on the hilt (no readable numbers).
- **Shot:** A night duel in a rain-soaked industrial port. The Saetta lunges with the plasma blade lit, rain flashing to steam around it, the light reflecting off wet concrete and cranes. Its opponent is half out of frame, cut through at the shoulder.

## Kirov-Mikoyan OKB-7

*Eurasian Pact*

**House style:** OKB-7 design language: huge aggressive heavy mech, angular slab armour, weapons built into forearms and back, heavy exhaust stacks, white and grey winter splinter camouflage, frost on the joints, red star markings.

### OB-21 Tsar

Prototype · 21.0 m · 138 t · Siege frame, the heaviest conventional frame ever built. Fires the Kalibr-305 mortar or carries the Iskra particle cannon fed by cable from an MTU-7 generator truck.

- **Silhouette:** Bigger and heavier than the Mamont: enormous slab-armoured torso, short massive legs, and a huge weapon on the back that dominates the outline. When firing it braces on four spades and looks like a self-propelled gun.
- **Head:** A small, heavily armoured head set low and forward, with a slit vision block and a large rangefinder housing on top.
- **Torso:** Thick angular slab armour with appliqué blocks. Twin D-40 diesel exhaust stacks rise behind the shoulders.
- **Arms:** Heavy arms with GSh-style forearm rotary cannons for self-defence, and large clamp hands for handling shells.
- **Legs:** Short, massive legs. Two hydraulic spades on the back of each leg drive into the ground, plus a rear outrigger.
- **Back & power:** Twin D-40 diesels. Two interchangeable weapon fits:
  - **Kalibr-305:** a 305 mm siege mortar, a stubby fat barrel on a heavy cradle over the right shoulder, with a shell hoist. A second mech carries the ammunition.
  - **Iskra:** a long, segmented particle cannon on the back with magnetic focusing rings, heavy cooling jackets and a thick armoured cable trailing to a wheeled MTU-7 generator truck. When it fires: a blinding blue-white bolt, ionised air and steam from the cooling jackets.
- **Colours:** White and grey winter splinter camouflage, red stars, frost on the joints, soot streaks behind the exhausts. Test-unit calibration markers on the weapon cradle.
- **Details:** Ice-packed spades, ammunition-handling stencils, crew ladders, a heavy tow bar on the rear.
- **Shot:** A frozen ridge at dusk overlooking a fortified city on the horizon. The Tsar is braced on its spades and fires the Kalibr-305: a huge muzzle blast and a shockwave flattening the snow. A second heavy mech beside it lifts the next shell from a sled. Generator trucks and support crews below.

### OB-X Pamir

Prototype · 20.4 m · 124 t · Fission-powered test frame. It can run for weeks. Its pilots' radiation exposure is a state secret.

- **Torso:** Thickened rear torso armour to shield the cockpit from the reactor. Coolant pipes run over the shoulders.
