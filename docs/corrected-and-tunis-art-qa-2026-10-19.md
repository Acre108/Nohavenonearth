## Practical limits

These are visually reviewed concept sheets, not measured orthographic engineering drawings. Some full-body studies retain perspective and minor panel/fastener variation; depicted human scales are approximate. Large mechanism and equipment corrections were checked visually. The shield and Ashvin chassis were rebuilt when edits to the old references repeatedly preserved or introduced defects.

Generation and edit chains are saved for the selected finals. Discarded intermediate images remain in the image tool's generation storage and are not linked as final project assets. No changes were made to the three source brief files.
