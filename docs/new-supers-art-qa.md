Limits: illustrative mechanical studies, not CAD or dimensionally exact engineering drawings. Scale labels express intended canon heights, but comparator silhouette proportions are approximate and sometimes inconsistent. Fine armour seams, small mechanisms and paint weathering vary between views. The Counterfeit source silhouette comparison also retains some salvaged-design detail rather than being a strict reconstruction of the intact parent. Existing entries 1–11 and optional alternate scenes were not regenerated in this pass.
