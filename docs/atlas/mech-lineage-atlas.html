        How to use the atlas

        Where to start

        New to the setting? The Timeline tells the whole story in order, from before the first mechs to 2327. Or open a faction's Summary from the bar under the title, and follow just its story through every tab.

        What's in each tab

        • StoryTimeline, Conflicts, Map and Factions: what happened, where, and what became of each power.
        • PeoplePersons of Interest and Aces: the families and the famous pilots behind events.
        • MachinesFrames, Supers, Vehicles, Fleets & Carriers, Weapons and Power: what everyone fights with, and where each design came from.
        • WorldOrganisations, Doctrine and Mysteries: who builds and decides, how each side fights, and the stories nobody has told yet.

        Reading a tree

        • Columns are generations, from Gen 0 before the first mechs to Gen 9 in 2327.
        • Rows are families: one maker, nation or line in each band.
        • Lines join each entry to what it grew from. The key above each tree explains the line styles.
        • Click any card to open its full entry, with links onward.

        Faction views

        Change view, in the bar under the title, filters every tab to one faction's story; the whole atlas shows everything. You can reopen this guide at any time from the Guide button in the same bar.
