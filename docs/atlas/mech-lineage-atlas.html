Select any entry to trace its lineage. Frames link to the power, weapons and doctrine they were built around. The world map shows where the blocs stood, and where they fought, in each era. The atlas covers the decades up to 2186 that build toward the Third World War, the war in which No Haven on Earth is set. The Factions tab follows each faction through the whole period, and the Successors tab shows what each one becomes by 2327.

        Where the space factions begin

        Most of No Peace Past Luna's colonial and outer-system factions start here as arguments about what a person may become: what one bloc did to its pilots, and what someone else learned from watching. Some start somewhere colder, or worse. Space doesn't carry Earth's politics outward unchanged; it mutates them, and each colony keeps its sponsor's argument while changing what it means. The Constitutionalists don't appear: they are built later, to replace Colony One. The top colour shows the Earth power each faction descends from; grey means none.
