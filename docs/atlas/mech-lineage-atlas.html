No Haven on Earth Atlas

The 22nd century is running on empty. Two hundred years of warming have drowned the coasts and thinned the harvests, and what's left is being fought over by three great power blocs: the United States, the Continental Union and the Eurasian Pact. For years they only stared each other down. Then, in 2162, four Garrisons came down a ravine at Iron Gate and destroyed a tank column in eleven minutes. The frame had fired the first shot, and the silence was over.

This atlas follows everything that came after: the frames and the arms race behind them, the wars they fought, the pilots who made them famous, and the road to the Third World War.

The whole story in order, from the Ares mission through the war and the First Martian Crisis of 2312 to No Peace Past Luna in 2327.           Where the space factions begin

          Most of No Peace Past Luna's colonial and outer-system factions start here as arguments about what a person may become: what one bloc did to its pilots, and what someone else learned from watching. Some start somewhere colder, or worse. Space doesn't carry Earth's politics outward unchanged; it mutates them, and each colony keeps its sponsor's argument while changing what it means. The Constitutionalists are built in No Peace Past Luna's third generation, after the First Martian Crisis, to replace Colony One, but the charter they are built on is written here.
