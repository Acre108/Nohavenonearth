# New frame artwork review — 19 October 2026

Reviewed all sixteen final PNGs listed in `output/imagegen/New-Frames-Art-Manifest-2026-10-19.json` against the descriptive briefs in the Gen 1, Gen 2 and Gen 3 art documents. This is a visual review; no artwork was altered.

## Overall assessment

The industrial rendering and maker palettes work, but the set needs a correction pass before it becomes a reliable design reference. The largest problems are incorrect weapons, inconsistent human scale, inherited winter details on the tropical Medved-N, and equipment changing between sheets and scenes. I should have caught these before reporting the set as finished.

Several sheets provide attractive perspective studies rather than true front/side/rear orthographic turnarounds. Their detail insets are useful, but panel geometry and equipment positions should not be taken as mechanically resolved.

## Priority 1 — correct before accepting the designs

### BD-2 Tundra — both images

- **Wrong gun.** The right-hand weapon is a multi-barrel rotary gun. The brief specifies the M30, a stubby single-barrel 30 mm chain gun with a pistol grip and box magazine. Correct the sheet first, then the scene.
- **Human scale is wrong on the sheet.** The parka figure is roughly one fifth of the mech's height, whereas a 1.8 m person beside a 17.2 m frame should be about one tenth. This makes the machine read as roughly half its intended size.
- **Scene weather misses the brief.** It shows a clear sunset and long visibility with blowing snow, rather than a blizzard obscuring an ice-road convoy. The snowy mountain road does not clearly establish a frozen lake.
- Keep the quilted covers, left pile bunker, cold-start drum, pre-heater and white-over-olive finish.

### Type-20 Tianyan — both images

- **Severe scale problem.** On the technical sheet, the scale figure comes almost halfway up the body rather than remaining near ankle height. In the scene, officers, tent and radar truck also make Tianyan read as a much smaller machine than 16.8 m.
- **Escort conversion changes between images.** The sheet's headless/plated conversion is a useful direction, but the scene restores prominent domed heads and gives escorts tall arrays resembling the command frame's equipment. Make the plated cockpit and replacement sensor block unmistakable; reserve the large Tianshu node and mast for Tianyan.
- **Wrong escort weapon on the sheet.** Its small escort study carries the drum-fed service rifle instead of the specified long Type-09 cannon. The scene does provide the long cannons.
- **Folded mast is not demonstrated.** The lower node studies show upright arrays rather than an unambiguous folded-versus-raised mechanism.
- Keep the scene's correct total of six numbered escorts plus one command frame.

### QF-6 Harmattan — both images

- **Wrong shoulder weapon.** The specified Type-01Q shoulder cannon has become a four-tube launcher in both images.
- **Shield changes shape.** The sheet shows a tall, relatively narrow arm shield, while the scene turns it into a huge bent, L-like structure. No hinges or deployment sequence explain the change. Define one broad curved dozer blade, wider than the shoulders, and use it consistently.
- **Medical bay moves.** The brief places it in the side of the hull; the scene opens a large central/front torso bay. Fix its location across views and scene.
- **Pose misses the brief.** The scene's mech is standing, rather than kneeling behind its planted shield.
- Human scale on the sheet is too large for a 17.6 m machine. Keep the water panniers, shelter activity, chalk drawings and medical stripe.

### BD-X Polaris — primarily scene, plus sheet scale

- **Airlock moves.** The sheet shows a rear hatch; the scene places the entrance on the visible side/front quarter. This materially changes the cab layout.
- **Scale is too small.** The nearby crew, human-sized hatch and cab glazing make it read as a considerably smaller vehicle than 16.4 m; the sheet's human figure is also oversized.
- **Rescue access is unresolved.** The sled meets a narrow staircase without a clear ramp or lifting arrangement. A winch-assisted stretcher lift or folding ramp would make the illustrated operation convincing.
- Keep the headless cab, two bunks, unarmed grippers, recovery winch and thermal plumbing. The roof mast is allowed by the brief and is not an extra head.

## Priority 2 — targeted corrections

### NK-12 Medved-N — both images

- **Winter imagery leaked into the tropical refit.** The sheet retains repeated icicle-like projections under armour and weapons. Some bright streaks in the scene can reasonably be water, but the persistent pointed forms on the dry sheet are inappropriate for the monsoon design.
- **Rotary barrel count needs correction.** Several muzzle faces appear to have seven openings, rather than the specified six barrels. Use a clearly readable six-barrel cluster.
- **Rocket layout needs standardisation.** The perforated grids vary between views and do not clearly establish the specified forty tubes. Two consistent twenty-tube banks would remove ambiguity.
- The sheet's scale figure is too large. Surviving white/grey Russian panels are not strongly distinguished from the khaki finish.
- Keep the bear-like mass, large radiator grilles, sealed rubber joints, horse insignia and factory scene.

### BD-7 Wolverine — sheet and minor scene refinement

- **Prone mechanism misses the brief.** The legs trail behind the hull in the technical prone view instead of visibly folding and tucking beneath it. Show the actual folding sequence and clearance.
- **Belly heat-sink is not explained.** There is no useful underside inset or clear demonstration of the plate contacting snow.
- The scene has netting but leaves much of the hull exposed. More drifted snow would better communicate a frame concealed in place for days. The foreground crew also weaken the impression of its enormous scale, though perspective makes a precise comparison uncertain.
- The dark passive sensors, absence of exhaust and orange bands, broad hands and snow-wrapped rifle are successful. This pair needs less work than Tianyan or Harmattan.

### NK-X Ashvin — both images

- **Head drifts back toward the Volk parent.** The side and three-quarter views retain a long projecting snout; the brief asks for a low wedge head with a horizontal sensor band.
- **Inner-shoulder data-link pods are not clearly identifiable.** They are central to the paired concept and need dedicated detail views.
- **Rail-rifle power equipment is unclear.** The copied capacitor housing is not clearly distinguished from the diesel/radiator pack; show its location and connection in a rear detail.
- The technical scale diagram is incorrect: its human is far too tall relative to an 18.6 m silhouette. The scene communicates scale more effectively.
- Keep the mirrored outer rifles/inner rotary cannons, white/black recognition stripes and removable harness handover. Those important requirements were achieved.

### BD-5 Caribou — relatively minor

- **Fjord is largely open water.** The brief calls for a frozen fjord; add continuous sea ice or substantial ice cover.
- The rounded head works, but the heated hood and mono-eye track could be made more explicit in a detail view.
- The twelve-cell pod is clearly established in the detail inset, but its small front-view grid is less consistent. Keep the same three-by-four arrangement throughout.
- This is one of the most consistent pairs: the mismatched head, right shoulder pod, left forearm winch, quilted joints, wide feet and supply sled all read clearly. Its sheet also has one of the better human scale references.

## Recommended correction order

1. Fix human scale on the affected technical sheets and in the Tianyan/Polaris scenes.
2. Correct Tundra's M30 and Harmattan's shoulder cannon.
3. Lock Harmattan shield/medical-bay geometry and Polaris rear-airlock placement.
4. Correct Tianyan escort conversion, escort weapon study and folding mast.
5. Remove Medved-N winter artifacts and standardise weapon counts.
6. Refine Wolverine folded legs/belly plate and Ashvin head/link/power details.
7. Apply scene weather/ice corrections and other small polish.

Correct the master sheets before regenerating dependent scenes, so existing errors are not propagated through the reference images again.
