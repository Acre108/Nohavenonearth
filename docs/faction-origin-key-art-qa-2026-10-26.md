All selected images were visually inspected after generation or editing. Named source images supplied mechanical design rather than scene setting. Scene scale remains illustrative and perspective-dependent; these are not measured engineering renders. The steppe train's travelling light wave is suggested in a still image. Minor generative equipment and panel differences remain.

Small details have practical limits: the American recruit's ear scar is not reliably readable; the rail crossing damage is distant and subtle; the river ledger is shown cover-up rather than open; some fruit remains freely floating in the CPS cabin; distant watchers have approximate scale. The Arctic child looks toward the pale horizon, but its body still faces the column. The Jinn's treatment scaffold raises the forearm above the sand rather than resting it flat on the ground. These points are recorded rather than presented as exact compliance.

One White Convoys edit was rejected at image-service output moderation with category `other`; a later edit succeeded. Its prompt and request ID are retained in the manifest. No deliverable remains blocked by that rejection.
