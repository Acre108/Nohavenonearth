# Art pass — 17 October 2026

Implemented against the original readability checklist and the local [art direction](art-direction.md). This pass changes rendering and presentation. Concurrent camera, movement-interface and rules work is preserved.

## Implemented

- Muted cel-painted armour, one hard key direction, cool shadow bands and thin ink hulls. Sensor View uses flat, unlit contact fills and faction outlines. Both read the global `sensor_mix` parameter.
- A Blender-built Blue mech replaces the ace's primitive mesh. The other procedural models have clearer proportions, weapons and stabilisers. Existing identities and loadouts are retained.
- Faction base rings, independent ground chevrons, one tether tick for Low and two for High, and progressively smaller cool ground shadows. Base cues follow the rendered body during movement.
- Ivory selection brackets, an ivory legal-target crosshair and neutral range sectors. Shape separates these in greyscale. Saturated hues are reserved for friendly cyan, hostile coral, draft amber and invalid magenta. The alternative target/invalid hues in the direction document remain proposals.
- Contour-shaded terrain, subdued architectural details, painted deployment aprons and readable terrain labels. Terrain positions, heights, footprints and rule flags are unchanged.
- World captions retain their authored pixel size through zoom; UI text is clamped to at least 12 px. Damage text animates its font size rather than its world scale.
- Sensor View while giving orders; Cinematic material mode during automatic resolution and shot effects. **Skip cinematic / F8** restores Sensor shading without advancing the turn. **Tactical only** persists between launches. The tactical camera stays under player control.
- A short expanding sweep from each living Blue unit on the existing commit callback. It does not gate information or delay the existing reveal.

## Review

Open `art/StyleTest.tscn` and run it with F6. The slider compares cel and Sensor materials; the checkbox shows black silhouettes. The front row compares five frames, and the rear row compares terrain and three altitude bands.

Run `powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\Test.ps1 -WithUI` for the rules, UI and art suites. To run art checks independently of concurrent interface changes:

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files (x86)\Godot\Godot.exe' --path . --script res://tests/test_art.gd
```

The 29 art checks cover caption size at both camera limits, altitude tick counts, facing agreement, automatic view selection, skipping without advancing the phase, and identical serialised battle state after presentation changes. Captures include Sensor, Cinematic, greyscale, default-camera silhouettes, the sweep and both style-test modes at 1080p. Output is in ignored `artifacts/art-*.png` and `artifacts/art-tests*.log`.

## Blender template

Source: `art/source/blue_line.blend`. Runtime mesh: `art/models/blue_line.glb`. Rebuild these and their silhouette evidence with:

```powershell
& 'C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 3.2\blender.exe' --factory-startup --background --python art/models/build_blue_line.py
```

The model has 1,800 triangles, 49 named rigid parts and six slots: `armour_primary`, `armour_secondary`, `frame`, `accent`, `sensor`, `weapon`. Its origin is ground centre. Blender +Y exports to Godot −Z. There are no textures or collision data. Six silhouette views and a model manifest are written to `artifacts/`. `.blend` source is excluded from Godot import, so play requires no Blender installation.
