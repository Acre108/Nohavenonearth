## Practical limits

These are concept-art sheets, not dimensionally verified orthographic plans. Small panel, seam and mechanism variations remain between views. Carbon-blade deployment and folding hinges are illustrated rather than mechanically resolved. Painted ornaments, crew markings and numbers are decorative; no readable graffiti copy was requested. The technical sheets for Bürged and Khar Salkhi contain small environmental motion insets in addition to their mechanical studies.

Existing ancestor art and source briefs were retained. The output manifest selects only the completed images; discarded draft generations remain in imagegen storage. No atlas deployment or website publication was performed.
