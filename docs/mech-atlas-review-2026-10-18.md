# Mech Atlas design review — 18 October 2026

Scope: all four local art-brief documents, the art indexes, the project art direction and README, with direct visual inspection of sixteen selected technical images across Gen 2, prototypes and Supers. The linked Claude artifact could not be opened through the web reader; no browser connection was available. This reviews the local atlas material, not the live artifact's layout or an independently verified copy of its latest text. Recommendations below are proposals; existing briefs, images and game data have not been changed.

## Overall assessment

The atlas has a useful foundation: manufacturers, power systems, licensed ancestors, depot upgrades and support machines give it more identity than a collection of unrelated hero robots. Preserve that structure. The main originality problem is the accumulation of recognizable features from one reference within a single machine or program. Generic ideas such as humanoid machines, mono-eye cameras, prototype rifles and pilot bonds can stay. A distinctive head, palette, silhouette, weapon set and narrative mechanism all pointing to the same reference need to be broken apart.

The most promising identity is **machines shaped by industrial ancestry and the institutions that operate them**. Osprey's battery handling, Mitsurugi's rescue ancestry, the orbital workers and the League's repair economy offer more distinctive directions than additional anime armour vocabulary.

## 1. Highest-priority originality changes

### Raijin: redesign the head and body together

The current technical sheet reads immediately as a Gundam: V-fin, red forehead block, twin eyes, white faceplate and chin, blue chest with yellow vents, red feet and waist, white limbs, heroic proportions and a beam rifle. This is also largely what the brief requests. Asking for a face that is not a Gundam face while retaining the surrounding combination will keep producing the same result.

Keep the name Raijin, Shiden ancestry, beam-weapon experiment, five-shot thermal limit and conspicuous cooling system. Those are enough to identify it.

Proposed replacement direction:

- Retain Shiden's horizontal binocular camera bar, recessed into a protective collar. No human mouth, chin, cheeks, forehead jewel or crown.
- Make the right shoulder and torso visibly braced for a large instrument-like emitter. Use a forearm cradle and left-hand support grip rather than an oversized infantry rifle silhouette.
- Put the heat-management module on one side of the back, giving it an unmistakable asymmetric outline. Include removable thermal cartridges and service access.
- Use pale grey, graphite and small orange test markings; retain the small red national disc. Remove the broad blue chest and red boots.
- Preserve rescue-derived precision hands, sealed lower-leg joints and stabilising feet. They should explain its descent from Mitsurugi service machinery.
- Remove the sword from the standard beam trial fit, or show it as a disconnected optional item. It distracts from the one technology being tested.

Suggested one-sentence identity: **A coastal-defence test frame carrying a beam instrument too large for its original chassis, kept operational by cooling crews and replacement thermal modules.**

### Halden: new designations, plus a modest conceptual shift

Changing Unit-00 to another short serial will help presentation but leave the deeper Eva association intact: an alien giant cloned into humanoids, restraint armour, violent activation, psychological pilot selection, a capsule inserted at the neck, a trailing power cable and precisely five minutes of reserve. The clinical white armour already helps differentiate the artwork; the control and deployment system needs equal attention.

Recommended designation system: **Halden H-series, with accession-style identifiers**. The H number records the Institute's culture accession, not a production sequence. The following are proposed identifiers, not pre-existing lore:

| Current entry | Proposed atlas display | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Halden Unit-00 | **Halden H-17 — First Growth** | First culture accession successfully matured to deployment |
| Halden Units 01–02 | **Halden H-24 / H-31 — Field Cohort** | Two separately tracked organisms using the same containment pattern |
| Unit-03 | **Halden H-46 — Growth Trial** | Uncommissioned organism; still held under a laboratory accession |
| The Jinn | **The Jinn** | Independent name; Halden records identify it as missing H-17 |

Avoid implying H-17 means seventeen completed giants. Explicitly establish that most accession numbers refer to failed, microscopic or nonviable cultures. Keep organism identity, restraint-harness revision and operational callsign separate. A repaired harness does not create a new organism.

Minimum accompanying changes:

- Move the operator to a conventional protected cab on the restraint frame, with a side access hatch. Replace the neck plug with distributed surface interfaces.
- Recast the cable as a perfusion/cooling and control bundle. The tissue supplies locomotion metabolically; the support equipment regulates it. This also gives the Jinn a meaningful independent maintenance problem.
- Replace the fixed five-minute battery countdown with a variable physiological limit: heat, accumulated metabolites or loss of containment control, depending on effort and damage.
- Make the restraint frame an external apparatus with visible load paths and joint brakes, closer to medical traction equipment than fitted human armour. Retain the mineralised tissue and faceless head.
- Give H-24 and H-31 different maturation and support needs. They can share a harness pattern without being identical organisms distinguished only by a painted number.

The Jinn's cooperation should have an observable cause beyond meeting the uniquely right young pilot: for example, the mechanic discovers that Halden's suppression cycle caused the distress it treated as aggression. Trust can still matter, but practical care gives the relationship its own identity.

## 2. Contradictions and missing mechanisms

### Power generation, stored energy and propulsion are mixed together

Kestrel has engine-nacelle calves and exhaust nozzles, yet its power description says it has no exhausts and is silent. A battery can supply electrical energy; it does not explain thrust or eliminate airflow and propulsion noise. Specify electrically driven ducted propulsion, another defined mechanism, or a separate fuel-burning flight system. Change the quiet claim to the condition in which it applies, such as stationary watch with propulsion off.

SKYLINE then needs an explicit statement of what beamed power replaces. If flight still consumes propellant, six hours without landing requires a propellant budget as well as an electrical supply. Define receiving orientation, beam loss behaviour and how a transforming body keeps the receiver exposed.

Every power entry would benefit from four short fields: **source, buffer, propulsion, heat rejection**. A capacitor is a buffer, not a sustained generator; solar collection is not automatically combat-thrust power.

### The Jinn has lost its support system, but not acquired a replacement

Halden organisms require grid power and brief reserves. The Jinn is the same first organism, operating independently in a desert without a stated alternative. This needs a sentence of actual lore. The proposed metabolic/perfusion distinction above resolves it: Halden powered the restraints and suppression apparatus, while the Jinn still needs water, feedstock and rest. Otherwise give it a visible mobile generator and explain who supplies it.

### Technology chronology is unclear

Vanguard is currently testing HT-20 turbine-capacitor technology while about a dozen operational Concords already use a scaled-up HT-20 plant. Takemikazuchi likewise carries a scaled BX-0 while Raijin tests the first frame designed around a beam weapon.

These can coexist, but only with explicit qualifications: giant installations came first and portable versions are experimental, for example. Alternatively move the supers later, or give their early installations separate designations. Gen 2/3 needs to indicate chronological era, maturity, or chassis generation consistently; it currently does several jobs.

### Unsupported dramatic assertions

- **Tieniu tips over when firing standing.** A requirement to kneel and deploy spades is a useful limitation; routine overturning makes a mass-produced combat vehicle implausibly unusable. State the safe firing envelope and describe overturning as an accident outside it.
- **Halden walks with knee clamps locked tight.** A locked joint prevents ordinary walking. Show the bands closed around the limbs while the articulated brakes remain released; reserve joint locking for containment or transport.
- **Unit-03 shows activity at night “which should not be possible.”** It is living tissue that already reacts to stimuli. Specify the violated expectation: coordinated movement during verified inhibition, activity without perfusion, or learned responses it was never taught.
- **Koschei pilots last a fixed number of sorties.** Sorties vary. Cumulative interface exposure or neural damage is a stronger mechanism, with a nominal sortie allowance imposed by officials.
- **Koschei-2 deletes extraction.** Explain access for installation, life support and replacement. A removable rear service assembly can exist while emergency pilot escape is deliberately omitted. This is more credible and more chilling than an entirely inaccessible consumable pilot.
- **Tsar is the heaviest walking frame “in the world.”** Supers elsewhere weigh much more. Qualify it as the heaviest conventional frame.

### Hardware needs visible support

- **Tieshan's 200 mm handheld cannon:** show its ammunition, loading method, recoil stroke and bracing. It can be a very short-engagement weapon, but the drawing currently communicates an enlarged assault rifle more readily than a large-calibre system.
- **Pamir and reactor carriages:** make heat rejection, shield direction and shutdown behaviour part of the design. Weeks of fuel endurance do not mean weeks without coolant, maintenance, ammunition or crew support.
- **50 m Koschei on a railcar:** use a disassembled shipment on several heavy transporters, or establish a dedicated oversize industrial railway. An ordinary rail-yard depiction needs clearance and loading constraints acknowledged.
- **Concord's “scaled-up parts”:** enlarge the design language, not every purchased component. Engines and actuators can be installed in banks of normal-size modules. Hatches, ladders, handles and fasteners should stay human-sized.
- **Choir's purchased parts:** a 38 m robot cannot simply wear 17 m shoulder and leg assemblies at twice their size. Make its superstructure newly fabricated around banks of bought components; retain recognisable donor panels locally.
- **Orbital anchors:** magnetic pads need suitable structure. Add mechanical truss clamps and docking shoes for nonmagnetic surfaces. Define the fuel and oxidiser/working-fluid arrangement separately from electrical power.
- **HEAT lance:** clarify expendable warhead tips, stand-off spacing, blast direction and reload procedure. Do not present a shaped-charge tip as an ordinary reusable sword point.

## 3. Designs that need stronger identities

| Design | Weakness | Strengthening direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Harrier** | Mostly Kestrel with more armour, missiles and a laser. | Make it a package-escort / counter-missile platform. Show sensors, coverage arcs and the power/cooling penalty of protecting other aircraft. |
| **Bulwark** | Mostly Teamster enlarged; the battery-site role is not strongly visible. | Give it deployable cover, ground anchors and swappable resupply/site-defence modules. Trade speed and transportability for sustained defence. |
| **Pavise** | Sniper plus EW leader makes it the best at too many unrelated jobs. | Link them through a scout/control role with passive targeting, or split the fits. High-power jamming should carry an exposure or precision-fire tradeoff. |
| **Tiebi** | A huge walking chassis dug in almost completely needs a reason to exist over a cheaper fixed emplacement. | Establish a mobile fortress belt: it moves between prepared firing cells, needs little site preparation and can leave after firing. Show search and fire-control equipment for its anti-air role. |
| **Falco** | Red/gold slim knight with a crest and lance reads as a general anime ace. “Italian sports car” is currently mostly decoration. | Show distinctive cooling ducts, shaped composite shells, exposed quick-service race fittings and measurable tradeoffs: short overhaul intervals, reduced protection or expensive specialist servicing. |
| **Saetta** | The sword and red knight dominate; strong arms “for reaction forces” are an unconvincing explanation for a free plasma blade. | Prefer a physical blade with a powered cutting edge, defined contact loads and a short thermal window. If a free plasma sword stays, explicitly classify its containment as a speculative breakthrough. |
| **Lantern Custom** | Matte black, mono-eye, erased serials, sword and long rifle are familiar mercenary shorthand. | Make the modification about an actual contract: boarding, urban recovery, deniable raids or counter-frame policing. Show the associated equipment. Removing transformation also needs a clear reliability/cost benefit. |
| **Shiden** | A fine test chassis, but not necessarily a separate battlefield design. | Label it as an instrumented service derivative. Empty universal mounts and recorder modules are its identity; it does not need another heroic redesign. |
| **Choir** | Eleven lenses stand in for an eleven-mind system; voting on every action leaves basic control unexplained. | Put stabilisation and reflexes in ordinary automation; let the minds vote on goals and permissions. Show redundant core bays, arbitration hardware and disagreement behaviour. Lens count should be a deliberate choice, not the sole proof of eleven minds. |
| **Koschei** | Effective silhouette, but unusually thin exposed limbs conflict with the emphasis on heavy armour. | Deliberately protect the capsule and weapon roots while treating the limbs as expendable, or thicken the load paths. Let that priority be visually legible. |

Keep and develop **Osprey**, **Kizuna**, **Lifter/Rigger/Marlinspike**, and the **League repair lineage**. They have clear functional reasons for their forms. For the League, add standard adapter rings, harness converters and software bridges so cross-bloc parts are an engineering practice rather than interchangeable by magic. Give it original solutions too: filtration, recovery gear and field-built power adapters, rather than only second-hand hardware.

## 4. Artwork and brief quality

The selected images show recurrent generation drift:

- Several human scale figures are too large relative to their stated 17–18 m frames. Use a fixed scale strip generated from the written dimensions, rather than trusting a decorative human silhouette.
- Enact and Tieshan are specified with particular sensors or hand structures, but images can substitute paired eyes and human-style fingers. Lock these features before requesting detail refinements.
- Some “technical profiles” are one three-quarter view with a weapon beside it. They do not establish rear access, joint clearance or transformation. Request a separate consistent front/side/back construction sheet.
- The Jinn's illustrated rider and cab make it feel substantially smaller than the stated 40 m. Restore scale using a verified 17 m escort and human-sized cab.
- Supers often use the same armour panel and bolt density as ordinary frames. Human-scale service infrastructure should establish their size even when the scale silhouettes are hidden.
- The sheets repeat dense chipped paint, tiny fasteners, exposed tubing and armour seams. More surface detail is not a substitute for a recognisable silhouette. Use larger quiet surfaces and distinct material/construction logic per maker.

The briefs also seed familiar designs with named anime references, then ask the image model not to copy them. Replace positive references with concrete sources: rescue manipulators, excavator undercarriages, aircraft access and conversion mechanisms, mobile cranes, pressure vessels, medical fixation frames and shipyard handling systems.

The older `docs/art-direction.md` still calls itself canonical while requiring Federation/Zeon-derived factions, banning transforming machines and using Newtype language. That conflicts with the atlas briefs and current roster. Resolve the hierarchy: retain its Sensor/Cinematic presentation guidance while marking its old faction and technology assumptions superseded.

## Recommended order of work

1. Set the canon hierarchy, power/propulsion terminology and technology timeline.
2. Rewrite Raijin and Takemikazuchi as distinct machines before regenerating art.
3. Adopt the Halden accession designations and revise its interface, tether and reserve mechanism together.
4. Rename Enact and Newtype terminology; separate Concord's crew concept from neural compatibility.
5. Strengthen the roles of Harrier, Bulwark, Pavise, Tiebi and Lantern Custom.
6. Fix scale, hand/sensor consistency, support hardware and coherent orthographic sheets.

Acceptance question for each design: **With the paint removed and the name hidden, can someone identify its maker, job, ancestry and major limitation from the shape?** If the main answer is the anime it resembles, it needs another design pass.
